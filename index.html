Maxov

DJ · Productor · Cofundador de PORTAL

Maxov, a 29-year-old DJ and producer from Montevideo, Uruguay. His journey behind the decks began in 2022. Since then, he has shared the stage with international DJs such as Anna, Adam Sellouk, Mila Journée, Metodi Hristov, Hozho, Nico Moreno, Beico, MT93, Xzyko, among others. His music flows from Melodic, setting the tone for an introspective experience, and evolves into Techno, adding strength, rhythm, and darkness to the pulse of the night.
